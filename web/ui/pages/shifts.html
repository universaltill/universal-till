Shift Management

Open Shift

Enter amount in pence (e.g., 10000 for £100.00)

Close Shift

Enter counted cash amount in pence

Cash Adjustment / Payout

Use negative values for payouts (e.g., -5000 for £50 payout)

Expected Cash Formula

Expected Cash = Opening Cash + Net Cash Payments + Cash Adjustments

  • Opening Cash: The starting cash in the drawer at shift open
  • Net Cash Payments: Sum of cash payments minus change given during the shift
  • Cash Adjustments: Payouts (negative) or adjustments (positive/negative) recorded during the shift

When closing a shift, the system calculates the expected cash and compares it with the actual counted cash to determine any variance.
