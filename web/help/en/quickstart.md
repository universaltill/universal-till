# How to use the till

Everything you need for a normal day, in small steps. You cannot break anything by trying — take your time.

## 1 · Start your day

1. Turn the till on. When the number pad appears, type your PIN.
2. You are in! The big screen with product pictures is where you sell.

## 2 · Sell something

1. Point the scanner at the barcode — beep — the item appears in the basket on the left.
2. No barcode? Tap the product's picture instead.
3. You can also type the name or code in the box at the top and press Add.

## 3 · Take the money

1. When everything is in the basket, look at the Pay area.
2. Tap Cash if they pay cash, Card if they pay by card. That's it — the sale is done.
3. Paying part cash, part card? Use the Split tab and add each payment.

## 4 · Receipt and the next customer

1. After paying, the receipt appears. Tap Print if the customer wants a copy.
2. Tap New customer and you are ready for the next person.

## 5 · Customer forgot something?

1. Tap Hold — the basket is saved and shown as a small button above the tabs.
2. Serve the next person. When the first customer returns, tap their saved basket to continue.

## 6 · The barcode won't scan

1. Type the first letters of the product's name in the box at the top — it will find it.
2. Or tap the product's picture on the screen.
3. If your till has the camera button 📷, tap it, take a photo of the product, and pick the right match.

## 7 · Opening and closing the day

1. Go to Shifts in the menu and tap Open shift — count the cash in the drawer and enter it.
2. At the end of the day, go back to Shifts, count the drawer again, and close the shift.
3. The till shows any difference for you — no maths needed.

## 8 · Change the language

1. Open **☰ Menu** and tap your language's own name near the bottom (for example Türkçe or فارسی). Everything changes at once.

## 9 · Something looks wrong?

1. Internet down? Keep selling — the till works without it and catches up later.
2. Tapped something by mistake? Nothing is lost — the basket stays until you finish or reset it.
3. Still stuck? Ask your manager, or open Help / FAQ in the menu for more answers.
