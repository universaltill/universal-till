# Languages & display

The whole till speaks English, Türkçe, فارسی and العربية — including right-to-left layout — and adapts to touch screens with an on-screen keyboard and adjustable text size.

## How to use it

1. Change the language from the menu; each user can pick their own.
2. Settings → Display: adjust the UI scale for your screen.
3. The on-screen keyboard pops up automatically on touch screens (or force it on/off in Display settings).
4. Settings → Tills: give this till its own name (e.g. "Front Counter") so it's easy to tell apart from other tills — shown on the Tills page and defaults to "Till 1" until you set one.
5. Settings → Tills: "This till's register" tells the till which register it is. With a single register it's picked automatically; on a shop with more than one register, choose it here — cash payouts (like a bottle-deposit refund) are recorded against this register's open shift, and the till will refuse to record a payout until it knows which register it is.
6. Settings → Shop type: the kind of business you picked in the setup wizard (café, retail, service trade, hospitality, market stall or other) — change it here any time.
7. Settings → Data → Sample data: if you loaded the starter catalogue during setup, remove it all here with one tap — sample items, the 3 sample customers, and the 3 sample promo codes together, so a discount code from the demo data doesn't stay redeemable after you've moved on. Anything already in real use is kept (your sales history stays intact, and a sample customer or promo you've actually started using survives) — the till tells you how many it removed and how many it kept. If a sample item or customer is currently in the till's basket (cashier or self-order), removal is blocked until the basket is cleared — clear it and try again.
8. Settings → Display also has a window-mode selector (normal, maximized, fullscreen, kiosk), a launch-on-startup toggle, and an "Exit to OS window" action next to them (asks for a manager PIN). On Linux desktop, the window-mode selector and Exit to OS window both apply immediately, no restart needed — fullscreen/kiosk hide the OS's own window chrome, and Exit to OS window un-fullscreens the till and hands control back to the desktop; launch-on-startup still applies the next time you start the till (adds or removes a real autostart entry). On a Raspberry Pi kiosk till, the window-mode selector is real too and applies immediately, no restart needed: switch it to "kiosk" and the dedicated kiosk screen turns on right away; switch it to anything else and it turns off (the Pi's launch-on-startup toggle stays scaffolding — the box already starts the kiosk screen on its own from boot); Exit to OS window has no effect on a Pi appliance — there's no OS desktop to leave, so the window-mode toggle is your way back. On a Pi appliance the kiosk screen is the only screen — switching away from "kiosk" closes it and there's no console behind it, so you'll need another device on the same network (or SSH) to switch it back. On macOS and Windows, all three settings are still scaffolding: the till saves your window-mode/launch-on-startup choice and accepts the Exit to OS window PIN, but nothing actually changes until that platform's support ships — restart the till to pick up a different window mode there in the meantime.
9. Picking a country in the setup wizard automatically fetches that country's free plugins (today: the matching language pack) in the background — no marketplace hunting required, and it works even if you're offline at the time, retrying on its own once the network's back. While one is still installing (or waiting to retry), Settings → Data shows a small note with a Remove button if you'd rather not have it — nothing is locked in.
10. Settings → Data → Clear transaction history: wipes ALL sales, shifts and invoices from the working till in one go (not just test ones) — meant as a one-time reset before going live, not something to do routinely. The catalog, users, settings and archived reports are kept, nothing is destroyed: the cleared records move into a dated reset archive (see the next step) and can be restored for as long as the till hasn't traded since. Typing "RESET" into the confirm field is required before the button does anything, and the action itself is recorded in the audit log.
11. Settings → Data → Reset archives: every time you clear transaction history, that batch lands here instead of vanishing. **Restore** (type "RESTORE" to confirm) brings a batch's sales, shifts and invoices back into the working till whole and exactly as they were — but only while the till has had no new transactions since the reset, and only if nothing the batch references (a product, stock location or customer) was itself removed afterwards, e.g. by sample-data removal, catalog cleanup or a GDPR erasure. **Delete permanently** (type "PURGE" to confirm) removes a batch for good — but a batch holding real trading history is protected by your country's retention rules and can't be deleted before its retention date, shown next to it instead of the delete button until then.
12. Settings → Data → Catalog cleanup: permanently removes inactive products that were never sold and have no stock history — anything ever sold stays, for your records. Preview first to see what would go, then type "CLEANUP" to confirm the removal.
13. Settings → Data → Erase a customer (GDPR): search for a customer by name, phone or email and erase their personal details on request. Their past sales are kept for your records, but no longer show who they belonged to — the sale itself isn't deleted, only the link to that customer's identity.
