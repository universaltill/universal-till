# Support partner

A **support partner** is a company that helps your shop with Universal Till, for example the reseller who set you up. Without one, Universal Till's own support helps you.

## Where to set it

The partner is chosen on **my.universaltill.com**, not on the till: open your shop and choose **Support partner** in the tree, right after **Shop profile**. If this till is registered with the cloud itself (not only through the main till), the link and QR code at the bottom of this page take you there.

- Only the shop's **owner** can connect or disconnect a partner. A manager or viewer can see the connection, read-only.
- To connect, the owner enters the **partner code** the partner gave them, checks the partner's name, and ticks the box to confirm before connecting.
- One connection covers the whole shop: every location and every till.

## What changes

- **When you connect:** the partner sees your shop's name, the names of your locations, each location's plan and subscription status (including a trial end date or a missed payment), and when you connected. It does not see your sales, receipts, customers or staff. Universal Till may pay the partner a share of what you pay; connecting does not change your price. The partner cannot create or search for shops.
- **When you disconnect:** your shop goes back to Universal Till's own support. You can disconnect at any time.

## Selling is not affected

A support partner never changes how the till sells. Checkout works as usual, including offline. Connecting or disconnecting needs an internet connection, but only on my.universaltill.com.
