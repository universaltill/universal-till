# Reporting a problem

Something wrong with the till? Send a report — typed, spoken, or with a screen recording — from any screen with the 🐞 button.

## How to use it

1. Click the 🐞 button in the left-hand menu (managers and admins only). A small panel opens in the corner — the till underneath keeps working, so you can reproduce the problem while you describe it. If it's covering something you need to see, drag it to a different spot by its title bar.
2. Type what went wrong, or record a voice note; you can also record your screen, or take a screenshot — press "Take screenshot" to attach a still image of what's on screen right now (you can add more than one). The till's recent warning and error logs are attached automatically.
3. Press Save report. The report is stored on the till straight away — even offline — and uploads to the cloud automatically once the shop is online.
4. The panel stays with you as you move between screens — and so does everything in it. Take screenshots on several different pages, keep a recording running while you show the problem, then press Save report (or Discard to throw the draft away). The panel never closes on its own; ✕ just hides it, and 🐞 brings it back with your draft intact. If a page fully reloads while you're recording (for example after changing the language), the recording stops at that point and what was captured up to then is kept.

You can also reach it from the ☰ Menu via the Report an issue tile.

On the Android till app, "Take screenshot" captures what the app is showing directly — no permission prompt. The voice note works too: the first time you record one, Android asks for microphone access; allow it once and later recordings start straight away (if you refused, turn it back on under the phone's Settings → Apps → Universal Till → Permissions). Screen recording, and taking photos with the camera, aren't available in the Android app yet.

Closing the panel with ✕ hides it — with your draft kept — until you press 🐞 again: it stays shut as you move between screens, and the Report an issue page no longer forces it back open. Discard is the button that actually throws a draft away (it asks first). A draft lasts for the current till session only; up to 12 screenshots fit in one report.

Wondering what happened to a report after you sent it? See [My reports](/help/my-reports) — every report this till has sent, with its current status.

If diagnostic mode is on when you send a report (see Settings → Diagnostic mode in [Languages & display](/help/display)), the report also carries a reference to that diagnostic session and its most recent technical events, so support can read the report next to the stream it belongs to. Nothing extra is sent when diagnostic mode is off.
