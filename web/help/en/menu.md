# The Menu screen

Tap **☰ Menu** on the sale screen to open a page of big touch tiles — one for each part of the till. Tap a tile to go there; tap **← Back to sale** at the top of the page — or **Till** in the nav rail, available on any page — to get back to selling. Deposit refund opens in place as a small form instead of navigating away.

## Administration

Set-up tiles you touch once and rarely return to — Country settings, Translations, the fiscal register/device page, Locations and Registers — sit behind one **Administration** tile, below the everyday tiles. Tap it to open a page listing them in three groups: Fiscal, Locations, Localization. Users, Kitchen stations and Bluetooth devices stay outside this tile: you manage those more often than a one-time setup.

## Tiles a layout plugin has hidden

A **layout** plugin (a salon layout, for example) can hide tiles a shop does not need — Tables or Kitchen stations, say — and rename or reorder others; every page is still there at its own address. The same plugin can also rename or reorder rows on the **Items** screen's own section list (for example, a salon's "Library" row reading "Services") — but it cannot hide a row there, since that screen has no "bring it back" recovery of its own yet. It can likewise reorder the four buttons in the side rail — Sell, Menu, Inventory, Orders — and rename Inventory or Orders (the salon layout, for example, lists Orders ahead of Inventory); on a kiosk or tablet screen the rail is icon-only, so a rename there reaches the accessible name a screen reader announces, not on-screen text — the rename becomes visible text only once the screen narrows to phone width, where the rail becomes a horizontal bar at the top. It can never hide a rail button, and Sell and Menu can never be renamed, since they are the only way back to the sale screen and to everything else. To see what is hidden on the Menu and bring a tile back, open **Settings → Hidden menu tiles**. Each entry names the plugin that hid it and has a **Restore tile** button; **Hide again** undoes a restore. That same page also lists any tile a plugin has renamed, given a new icon, moved or regrouped without hiding it, naming which plugin made the change and what it replaced. The fiscal, Journal, Settings, Plugins, Help and Report-an-issue tiles can never be hidden by a plugin.
