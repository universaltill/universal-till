# Guided tour of the sale screen

The first time each person signs in to the till, the sale screen starts a short guided tour. A balloon points at one part of the screen at a time and says what it is for. It takes about a minute, and nothing you do in the tour changes a sale.

## Moving through the tour

1. Tap **Next** to go to the next part of the screen, or **Back** to see the previous one again.
2. Tap **Skip tour** at any time to close it and start selling straight away.
3. On the last step, tap **Done**.

With a keyboard, the arrow keys move forward and back and Esc skips the tour. The status bar and the Lock button keep working while the tour is open.

## What the tour shows

- **Menu** — the side menu (on a phone, the ☰ button) with stock, orders, reports and settings.
- **Products and categories** — pick a category, then tap a product to add it.
- **Find a product** — search by name; a barcode scan works at any time.
- **Basket** — what the customer is buying, with the running total.
- **Take payment** — the Pay button.
- **Open orders** — sales you put on hold.
- **Connection** — whether this till is online. Selling never stops when it is offline.
- **Help** — the manual for the screen you are on.
- **You and the lock** — who is signed in, and the Lock button.

A part that your screen does not show — on a phone, or for your role — is left out of the tour.

## Taking the tour again

Once you finish or skip the tour, it does not start by itself again for you. Each person on the till gets it once. To see it again:

1. Open **Help** — from the sale screen, the **?** button.
2. Tap **Take the tour**. It is on the Help home page, on the Selling & checkout page and on this page. The sale screen opens and the tour starts from the beginning.
