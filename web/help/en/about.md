# About, privacy and licences

The About screen shows the installed version and links to the privacy notice, the terms, the open-source licences and how to delete your Universal Till account.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings → About** and tap **Privacy, terms, licences and account deletion**, or tap the same link at the bottom of the manual's topic list. Every signed-in person can open it, cashiers included.
2. Tap **Privacy notice** or **Terms** to read them on the Universal Till website. They open in a new tab, or in the device's browser on Android. On a till that cannot open web links (a kiosk) the About screen shows the addresses instead, to type on another device.
3. Tap **Open-source licences** for the list of open-source software the till is built from, with each licence. It opens inside the till, with a **Back to About** link. The list covers the Go libraries the till is built from, the Go runtime and the front-end code shipped with the till. The libraries of the Android and iOS apps are not on it yet.
4. Tap **Delete your account** to open the page on the Universal Till website where the shop owner deletes their Universal Till ID. Nothing on the till is deleted by it.

These links are there on every version of the app, however it was installed.
