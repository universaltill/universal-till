# My display

Pick your own theme and sale-screen layout. They follow you to every till in the shop while you're signed in. Everyone else keeps the till's choice.

To open it, go to any back-office page (Settings, Users, Reports and so on) and choose **My display** in the top bar or menu. On the sale screen and the Menu it is not in the side rail; on a phone it is in the menu drawer on every screen.

## What you can set

- **Theme** — how the till looks for you. The list is the same as Settings → Theme: the built-in looks plus any theme plugin installed on this till.
- **How items are browsed** — your own sale-screen layout. A short note under the list explains the option you've picked.

Each choice has its own **Save** button. A new theme shows as soon as the page reloads.

## Use the till's choice

The first option in each list is **Use the till's choice**, with the till's current theme or layout in brackets. Pick it and press **Save** to clear your own choice; you then see whatever the till uses, and follow it when the shop changes it.

## Who can use it

By default admins, managers and owners can. Cashiers can't: the sale screen stays the same for everyone at the counter. An admin can change this under Users → Permissions, row **Personal display**.

## Good to know

- Your choice follows you to every till you sign in to in the same shop.
- On an additional till (one that follows a main till) the lists are switched off and a note says so. Make the change on the main till; it reaches the additional till automatically.
- If a theme you picked is later removed, the till's theme is shown instead and a note on this page tells you. Pick another theme, or **Use the till's choice**, to clear the note.
- Changing the till's own theme in Settings → Theme doesn't change what you see while you have your own theme set.
