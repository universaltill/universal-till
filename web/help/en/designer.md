# Receipt & screen designer

Customise what your receipt shows (logo, header, footer) and arrange the sell-screen buttons to fit how you work.

## How to use it

1. Open the receipt designer to change the logo and receipt text; changes preview live.
2. Use the screen designer to arrange quick buttons for your best-selling items.
