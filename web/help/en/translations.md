# Translation editor

Open **Menu → Translations** (managers only) to change how any label or message on the till is worded, in any of the till's languages. Pick a language, find the text, type your version — a cleared entry goes back to the built-in wording.

There are thousands of texts in the till, so the list loads a page at a time and fetches more automatically as you scroll down. Searching always looks through the whole list, not just what's already loaded.
