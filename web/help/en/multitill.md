Run several tills on the same shop network: one is the main till, the others join it and share the catalog, prices, settings and stock automatically.

1. Install the till app on the second device and choose Join on its setup screen.
2. Enter the main till's address (shown in its Settings) to pair them.
3. Sales made on any till flow back to the main till, and catalog changes spread to all tills within about half a minute.
