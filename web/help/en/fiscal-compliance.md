# Fiscal compliance (Germany)

German shops that record real sales need a technical security device (TSE). The till stops sales until one is set up, and the owner can allow sales for a limited time if a set-up TSE fails.

## The two switches

Both are in Settings → Fiscal compliance, and only the owner (admin) can change them:

1. **This shop records real sales** — leave it off while you try out the till (trial, demo, or running alongside your old till). Turn it on once this till is the real record of your sales. Every change is written to the audit trail.
2. **A TSE is set up for this shop** — turn it on once your TSE is in place, whichever way you got one (TSE hardware, your own cloud TSE account, or a managed TSE subscription).

## What the till stops

If your shop's country is Germany and it records real sales but no TSE is set up, the till will not complete a sale — at the register or at a self-order kiosk — until a TSE is set up. There is no way around this, on purpose.

Sales while you are only trying the till out (first switch off) are never stopped, and losing your internet connection never stops checkout either.

## If your set-up TSE stops working

The owner can allow sales for a limited time (up to 8 hours) from Settings → Fiscal compliance:

1. Enter a reason — what happened, in your own words.
2. Type the confirmation phrase exactly as shown.
3. Choose how long, in minutes.
4. If you are not signed in as the owner, enter the owner's PIN.

While the window is active, a warning stays visible at the top of the till. Every sale in the window is marked in the journal and on its receipt, so your records show exactly which sales were taken during the failure. When the time runs out, sales stop again on their own — nobody has to remember to switch anything back.
