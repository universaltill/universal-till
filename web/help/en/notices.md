# Notices

Messages from Universal Till and your partners arrive in a bell in the left-hand menu. They never get in the way of a sale.

## Turning it on

1. Open Settings and find the Notices card.
2. Tick "Show notices on this till" and press Save.

Notices are off by default. While they are off there is no bell and the till asks the cloud for nothing extra. Turning them off again removes the notices this till holds. A till only receives notices while it is connected to the cloud and its shop has an active plan.

## Reading notices

1. The bell shows how many notices are unread; "9+" means nine or more. A square red badge with a triangle means one of them is critical.
2. Tap the bell to open the inbox. Unread shows what you have not opened yet; All shows everything the till holds.
3. Tap a notice to open it. Opening it marks it read. Dismiss hides it from Unread; it stays in All until it expires.

Every notice says who sent it. A notice from Universal Till shows the Universal Till name; a notice from one of your partners has a dashed outline and "From" and the partner's name. Text in a notice is shown as plain text with bold, italic and safe links only.

## Pop-ups and the critical strip

- Info notices only change the bell. They never pop up.
- An important notice pops up once, at the top of the product grid, for about ten seconds. It pauses while you touch it. It never appears while the payment panel is open; it waits until you are back at the basket.
- A critical notice from Universal Till stays as a strip across the top of every screen until a manager or admin presses Acknowledge. Acknowledging does not mark it read.

None of these ever blocks, covers or delays a sale, a payment or a refund. The till keeps working offline.

## Who can see them

Managers and admins see the bell and the inbox. A cashier does not, unless an admin gives the role the Notices permission under Users, Permissions. A cashier without it still sees a critical notice from Universal Till as a read-only strip that says a manager has been told and selling continues.
