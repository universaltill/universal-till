# Alerts & notifications

The till watches your business and warns you early: items about to run out, days of stock left, order suggestions, and unusually high or low sales days.

## How to use it

1. The **Back office** screen is the daily glance: today vs yesterday, the week, what's running low, and what went wrong — with links into the full pages for the details.
2. Inventory shows days-left and order suggestions; Reports carries a low-stock chip.
3. A registered till also sends a daily digest to your online My shop inbox on the marketplace.
4. Unusual sales days (much higher or lower than that weekday normally) are flagged automatically.
