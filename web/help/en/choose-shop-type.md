# Choose your shop type

Your shop type tells the till what kind of business you run, so the Menu and Settings lead with what that kind of shop uses every day. A barber does not need Tables or Kitchen stations; a cafe does not need appointment-style wording. It is a tidy-up, not a restriction.

## What a shop type does

- **Hidden means tucked away, never disabled.** A Menu tile or a setting a shop of your kind rarely uses is moved out of the way: the Menu tile goes behind **Settings → Hidden menu tiles**, and the setting moves to **More settings** (the last tile on the Settings home), marked "Usually tucked away for this shop type". It works exactly the same, every page is still at its own address, and searching Settings finds it either way.
- **It may suggest a few starting values**, for example the dine-in/takeaway prompt for a cafe. You choose which, if any, to apply. Anything you have already set yourself is kept and never overwritten.
- **It never touches payments, tax, staff or PINs.** Those are never changed by a shop type.
- **A cashier sees none of this.** It is for managers.

## Pick it in the setup wizard

The setup wizard asks once, with one large tile per kind of business: retail shop, cafe or takeaway counter, restaurant or bar, barber, salon or service, market stall or pop-up, or something else. Pick one and press the next button, or press **Skip for now**: the choice is optional and you can make it later in Settings.

![The shop type tiles](img/choose-shop-type-tiles.png)

### An optional suggestion

When the plugin store is reachable and has a plugin made for the shop type you pick, a "Suggested for your shop" box appears under the tiles with its name and description. It is never ticked for you: tick **Install this when setup finishes** if you want it, leave it alone if you do not. Skipping leaves nothing behind, and you can always add the plugin later from the plugin store. If the till is offline, no suggestion is shown.

## Change it later

1. Open **Settings → Shop type** and press **Change shop type** (or **Change** under the tile grid on the Settings home).
2. Choose the new type and press the next button.
3. Read the preview. It lists what will appear in your Menu, what will move to **More settings**, the starting values you could also change (none are changed unless you tick them) and what is kept as you set it. Nothing has changed yet; **Back** leaves everything as it was.
4. Press the confirm button to apply it. Changing the shop type asks for a manager PIN.

## More settings

The Settings home is a grid of tiles, one per group of settings. The last tile, **More settings**, lists every setting in one place under the same group headings, including the technical ones and anything your shop type tucked away.

![The Settings home grid](img/choose-shop-type-home.png)

## Undo for 7 days

After a change, a strip appears above the Menu tiles and the Settings tiles (managers only) offering **Undo** for 7 days. Undo puts the Menu and Settings back as they were before the change, and reverts only the starting values that change applied: a value you edited yourself afterwards is left alone. The strip can be dismissed; the change stays undoable from it until the 7 days end.

![The undo strip](img/choose-shop-type-undo.png)

## A shop that was set up before shop types

If your shop existed before shop types, nothing changes by itself. Settings shows a one-time offer to tidy up for your shop type, with the preview first. Choose **Not now** to be asked again later or **Never** to stop being asked.

## Several tills

The shop type is a shop-wide setting decided by the main till. On an additional (satellite) till the card shows the type but has no change button: change it on the main till, and the satellite follows.
