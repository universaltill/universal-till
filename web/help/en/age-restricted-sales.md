# Age-restricted sales

Some items may only be sold after a member of staff has checked the
customer's ID — for example alcohol, tobacco, vapes or knives. You decide
which items these are: the till ships no list of products and no minimum
age. Once an item is flagged, the till reminds the cashier to check ID every
time it is sold, and keeps a record of what the cashier decided.

## Flag an item as age restricted

1. Open **Catalog** and select the item (or start a new one).
2. Tick **Age restricted (ID check at the till)** next to the other item
   options, such as *Sold by weight*.
3. Save the item. Every variant of the item is restricted too.

## At the till: the ID check

1. Ring the item up as normal. Its line in the basket shows a small badge
   reading **Needs ID check** straight away, so you can check ID before the
   customer is ready to pay.
2. Tap the badge. A small sheet opens with two buttons: **Accept** (you
   checked ID and the sale may go ahead) and **Refuse** (you will not sell
   the item).
3. After **Accept**, the badge reads **ID checked** and you can take payment
   as usual.
4. After **Refuse**, the sheet tells you to remove the item. Remove the line
   with its ✕ button and carry on with the rest of the sale. If the customer
   then shows valid ID, tap the badge again and choose **Accept** instead.

If you try to take payment while a restricted item still needs an ID check,
or was refused, the till does not complete the sale: it explains why and
opens that item's ID-check sheet for you. Nothing is charged and the basket
stays as it was.

The ID check belongs to one customer: it is cleared when the sale is
completed or the basket is cleared, and a parked (held) order asks again
when it is resumed.

## On the self-order kiosk

A self-order kiosk cannot check anyone's ID, so it will not take payment for
an order that includes an age-restricted item. The customer sees a message
asking them to pay at the counter, where staff will check ID. Nothing is
charged and their basket is left as it was. In pay-at-counter mode the order
is simply sent to the counter as usual, and the cashier's ID check applies
when it is paid at the till.

## What gets recorded

Each ID check is saved with the sale it belongs to: the item, its name at
the time, whether it was accepted or refused, which staff member answered,
and when. A refusal is kept even when the refused item was removed and the
rest of the sale went ahead. The record is written together with the sale
itself, so there is never a check without a sale.

These records are part of your shop's history. **Clear transaction history** (Settings → Data) archives them together with the sales and brings them back if you restore that archive, and **Catalog cleanup** and **Remove sample data** keep any item an ID check was recorded for, even one that was only ever refused.

## What this does and does not do

The ID-check prompt is a tool that supports your shop's own age-verification
policy — for example a rule that staff ask for ID from anyone who looks
younger than a set age. It does not, by itself, establish that your shop
meets any legal requirement. The
till does not know the law in your country, does not decide anyone's age,
does not check whether an ID document is genuine, and does not replace staff
training. Which items to flag, which age applies and which documents to
accept remain your shop's decisions.
