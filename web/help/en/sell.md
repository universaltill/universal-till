# Selling & checkout

The main register screen: scan or pick items, take payment, print or skip the receipt. It works fully offline — a network outage never blocks a sale.

## How to use it

1. Scan a barcode, or search / tap the item on the sell screen.
2. Adjust quantity or remove lines in the basket, then choose Pay.
3. Hold a basket to serve the next customer and recall it later; refunds are under the sale history.
