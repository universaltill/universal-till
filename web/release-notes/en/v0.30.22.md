## New

- **Join your shop's main till:** a new till that finds your shop's main till on the same network now offers managers to link to it. Give the till a name and tap Link this till, or tap ✕ to stop being asked on this till.
- **Name your shop:** until your shop has a real name, managers see a "Name your shop" notice at the top of the screen (never on the sell screen) with a Go to Settings link, so your receipts show your real name.

## Improved

- **Safer plugins:** a plugin can no longer take over the till's own pages, and plugin pages only open for viewing.

## Fixed

- **Sell screen buttons:** after going to the Menu and back, the Scan with camera, Identify by camera and Keyboard buttons next to the barcode box now stay put.
- **Pair with a shop:** entering a pairing code from your shop's online account under Settings → Pair with a shop now works; before, every attempt failed.
- **Hidden menu tiles on an additional till:** restoring or re-hiding a tile under Settings → Hidden menu tiles on a till that follows a main till now sticks; before, the next sync quietly undid it.
