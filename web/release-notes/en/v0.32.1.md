## Fixed

- **Selling gift vouchers with a German TSE:** when the German tax plugin signs your sales, a sale with a gift voucher in it was refused at payment. The voucher is now sent to the signer, so the sale is signed and completes. A voucher for any purchase is signed as not taxable. A voucher for one specific item is signed at that item's VAT rate. Update the German tax plugin to 0.12.0 as well.
