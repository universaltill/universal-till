## New

- **Order tracking QR on counter receipts:** on Shop plans and up, a sale rung up at the till can print a "Scan to track your order" QR on the receipt and show it on screen after payment. Switch it on in the receipt designer (**Order tracking QR (paid plans)**). Reprints reuse the same link.
- **Gift voucher numbers as barcodes:** every gift voucher sold now prints on the receipt with its value, who it is for, and its number as a barcode. Scanning the receipt later spends the voucher like a card. A voided voucher prints marked as void and without a barcode.

## Fixed

- **Self-order kiosk, pay at the counter:** orders paid at the counter now get a tracking QR too (Shop plans and up). Until the order is paid, the link shows "Waiting for payment at the counter".
- **Basket discounts and specific-item vouchers:** a basket discount no longer changes the VAT of a gift voucher sold for one specific item. The day-end report, invoices and receipts agree.
- **Plugin settings:** a plugin that isn't installed no longer shows an empty settings page.
