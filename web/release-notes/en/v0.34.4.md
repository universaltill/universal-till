## New

- **Correct a gift voucher's balance:** managers can now fix a voucher's balance from its detail page with **Correct balance**. Enter the new balance and a reason, then save. No money changes hands: the change, your name and the reason are recorded in the voucher's history and as their own line on the end-of-day report, and the correction is never counted as a sale or a payment. A voided voucher, or a voucher for a specific item, can't be corrected.

## Fixed

- **A till connects to the cloud only once your shop has a name:** a till no longer registers with the cloud while the shop is still unnamed. **Register now** in Settings says to name your shop first and links to the Shop name card, and a tax plugin chosen during setup is installed as soon as you save the name.
- **No false warning when a plan runs out:** if your plan ran out just as the till was sending its catalogue or order tracking to the cloud, the till could list that as a problem. It no longer does; it simply sends again once the plan is renewed.
