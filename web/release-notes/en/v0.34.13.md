## Improved

- **Plugin settings check what you type:** a plugin's settings page now shows on/off switches, number, percent and money fields, and checks every value before saving. A wrong entry is marked with a message in your language, and what you typed stays in the form. Nothing is saved until every field is right.
- **Decimal key in your language:** on the on-screen number pad, the decimal key now types a comma in prices and percentages when your till's language uses one, for example German or Turkish.
- **Receipt designer in your shop's language:** the sample ticket in the receipt designer now prints its labels (receipt number, Subtotal, Tax, TOTAL, Cash, Change) in your shop's language, the same as a real receipt.

## Fixed

- **Persian receipts line up:** on printed receipts, a label with a half-space (such as Persian "باقی‌مانده") no longer leaves its amount out of line.
- **Report retention dates:** in **Settings → Report retention**, the "Records held" dates now show in your language's date format and the right order, including in Persian and Arabic, and older reports' dates too.
- **Category filter:** while the category filter on the Catalog and Inventory pages is open, the Tab key stays inside it instead of reaching the page behind.
