## Improved

- **Less clutter after a crash:** if the till stops in the middle of an upload or import, the temporary files it left behind are now removed automatically after a day.
- **A leaner activity log with busy plugins:** when a plugin sends many updates in a short time, the till now records them as one summary entry per minute instead of one entry each, so the log stays small and fast.

## Fixed

- **Backups tell you when photos are missing:** if **Back up now** saves your data but can't add the photos, the till now says so and asks you to try again, instead of showing "Backup created" as if everything were in it.
- **Plugin panels stay with the people allowed to see them:** a plugin panel meant for Reports, Settings or Administration can no longer be opened directly by someone who isn't allowed on that page.
- **Quick taps between screens show the right page:** tapping two menu entries in quick succession no longer leaves the till showing one screen's content under another screen's name.
