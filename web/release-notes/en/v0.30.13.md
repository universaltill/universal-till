## Fixed

- **Phones:** every page now fits the screen. Wide lists (stock, shifts, translations and others) scroll sideways inside their own box instead of moving the whole page. The ☰ menu shows every item as a full row with its name. The Items section list no longer stays on top of the catalog while you scroll. In the basket, − and + sit either side of the quantity, the ✕ is next to the item name, and **Dine in / Takeaway** no longer breaks a word in two.
- **Report an issue** now appears for exactly the people allowed to report issues, on the Menu and in Settings.
- Plugins on phones and tablets no longer miss sale and stock events while a long import or export is running.
