## New

- **Plugin settings you can fill in without guessing:** a plugin can now show its settings with plain names, a short explanation, and drop-down lists instead of free text. Fields that don't apply to your choice are hidden. In the AI Assistant you pick the AI from a list (On my own network, ChatGPT, Claude or your own compatible server), and the server address and API key only appear when the choice needs them. A stored key shows **✓ Saved**, and you can remove it. Needs the AI Assistant plugin version 2.4 or later.
- **Remove a photo's background from the photo picker:** when your own background-removal service is set up, the item, category and variant photo pickers offer **Remove background** for a new photo. Nothing is sent until you tap it.

## Improved

- **Safer connection between tills:** once a till has been paired with its main till securely, it only talks to it over an encrypted, verified connection on your network.

## Fixed

- **Large amounts in Indian number format:** amounts of 100,000 or more shown by the screen itself (such as a fee hint or a split-payment message) are now grouped the same way as the basket for English (India) and Urdu (Pakistan).
- **AI Assistant notice:** the notice above the API key now describes correctly what Claude and your own compatible server receive.
