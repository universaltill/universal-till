## New

- **One Tills page:** All your tills and devices are now listed on a single page in Settings, so you no longer have to look in several places.

## Improved

- **Backups:** Backup problems that came from the cloud side are fixed, so offsite backups connect reliably again.
- **Clearer messages:** When a till cannot reach your shop's cloud account, the message now says plainly what happened and what to do next.
- **Restoring a till:** A till you restore from a backup now rejoins your existing shop. It never creates a new, empty shop by mistake.

## Fixed

- **Phone pairing:** Fixed several problems when pairing a phone with a till.
