## New

- **A renewed plan reaches the till by itself:** when a till's plan has run out, it now checks with the cloud every hour, so a renewal picks up on its own with no update or restart. Managers also get a **Check now** button on the "Paid features are paused" banner and on the Subscription card; it shows "Paid until …", "still not renewed" or "offline".

## Improved

- **Much smaller Android download:** each Android device now gets the package built for its own processor, about 38 MB instead of 150 MB. Your next update still comes as the large package once; after that the till moves to its own one.
- **Plugins are checked against the marketplace's own key:** the till now carries the marketplace's signing key built in and trusts only that key. A plugin file that isn't signed by the marketplace is refused, with a message that says why.

## Fixed

- **Android updates install reliably:** pressing Download now always starts the update, shows its progress, and opens Android's installer when the download is complete. A second tap no longer starts it again, a finished download is reused, and a download that stalls says so instead of sitting on "Downloading".
- **A self-order satellite stays a kiosk when staff sign in:** on a satellite in self-order mode, a manager's PIN at the kiosk lock opens Settings for maintenance and the device stays a kiosk; a cashier's PIN is refused. Locking it, or the idle lock, returns it to the self-order screen instead of the staff keypad.
- **Printed receipts in your shop's language:** the labels on a printed receipt (totals, tax, service charge, payment methods, change, tip, refund and the fiscal lines) were always printed in English. They now follow your shop's language, as the printed invoice already did.
