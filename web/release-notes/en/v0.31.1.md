## New

- **Update another till from the main till:** on the main till's Tills page, a manager can press **Update now** for any joined till that is behind it, even if automatic updates are switched off on that till. The joined till downloads and installs exactly the version the main till runs, as soon as no sale is open on it; if the update fails, press **Update now** again to retry.
- **Plugins can take files:** a plugin page can now ask you to choose a file, such as a photo, up to a size the plugin sets.
- **Plugins can run on a schedule:** a plugin you have given the schedule permission can now run small tasks on its own at an interval the plugin sets — as often as every half-minute. It waits while sales are being completed, never slows down a sale, and stops as soon as you disable or remove the plugin or take the permission away.
- **Plugins can add panels to the main screens:** a plugin can now show its own panel on Reports, End of day, Settings, Administration, the item edit window and the last step of first-time setup. If the plugin is slow or fails, the screen opens without its panel.
- **New "device information" permission:** a plugin you give this permission can read this till's device ID, its addresses on your local network and its time zone — some tax services require them. Without the permission it gets nothing, and every read is recorded in the till's audit trail.

## Improved

- **Backups include your photos:** every backup now also holds the photos you uploaded for items and categories and your receipt logo. Restoring a backup — even on a new device — brings them back, and the one file you download is the whole backup.

## Fixed

- **Older till registrations keep their cloud sign-in:** a till still on an older shared registration now receives its own sign-in from the cloud even while the shop's subscription is not active, so it reconnects by itself once the subscription is active again instead of having its sign-in refused later.
