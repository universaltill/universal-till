## New

- **Shop name in Settings:** change your shop's name any time under Settings → Shop name. A name saved on the main till also shows on the shop's cloud manage page after the till's next check-in, and the self-order screen uses it straight away.

## Improved

- **Phone sale screen:** search and edit sit in the top bar, Dine in / Takeaway sits next to Pay, and the categories are a row of rounded buttons that stays put while the items scroll. The status row moves into the ☰ menu and comes back above the bottom bar when something needs your attention, such as being offline.
- **Erasing a customer:** Settings → Data now explains that invoices already issued keep the buyer's details exactly as issued, because tax law requires you to keep them.

## Fixed

- **Tax rate changes:** a new tax rate applies straight away on every sale screen, including self-order. An invalid rate is refused.
- **Setting up a German shop:** the tax-rate plugin you agreed to stays queued until it is available, instead of being dropped without a warning.
- **Logs and bug reports:** passwords and keys are hidden in every log and bug report, not only in the log file.
