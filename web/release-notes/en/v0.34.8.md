## New

- **The till asks when a scanned code means more than one thing:** if a code you scan or type matches more than one thing, for example an item's barcode that is also a gift voucher number, the till no longer guesses. A short list at the top of the basket asks which one you meant (item, gift voucher, customer, promotion or receipt), and nothing is added until you tap a row. A code that matches only one thing works exactly as before.

## Improved

- **Warning before linking a till that already has sales:** when a till that already has sales finds a main till on your network, the notice now warns that linking replaces everything on it (sales, products and settings) with the main till's copy, and that its own sales are not sent to the main till. **Link this till** only works once you tick **Replace this till's sales and data**.
- **Every till uploads its own end-of-day reports:** when your reports are kept in the cloud, additional tills now upload their own archived reports too. The cloud stores them under that till, separately from the main till's, and each till shows how many of its own reports are still waiting to upload.
- **Easier item photos on iPhone and iPad:** the **Item image** tab now has a single **Add photo** button. It opens the device's own menu, where you can pick from your photo library, take a photo with the camera or choose a file.
- **New security key when a till moves to new hardware:** if a till's whole data folder is copied to another machine, for example by cloning its SD card or disk, the till notices and renews the security key of the connection between your tills, so two machines never share one key. You don't need to pair your tills again.

## Fixed

- **Android update no longer stuck on “Opening Android’s installer…”:** if Android's installer closes without installing, or never appears, the update button now says **Didn’t see Android’s installer? Tap to open it again**. Tapping it reopens the update you already downloaded, without downloading it again.
- **“Main till not reachable” shows sooner after a normal shutdown:** when the main till is shut down normally, an additional till's status bar now says **Main till not reachable — selling offline** within about 12 seconds, instead of after about a minute. Selling carries on as always.
