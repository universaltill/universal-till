## New

- **Restore from a file:** Settings → Backups now has "Restore from a file". Choose a backup file from a USB stick, another computer or your Downloads folder. The till checks the file and shows the shop, the number of sales and the newest sale before you restore it. A new till also offers this right after setup, so a till that broke can be replaced without starting again.

## Improved

- **Re-joining keeps unsent sales:** when a till joins a new main till, it first sends the new main till any sales it has not sent yet. If that fails, the join stops and the till keeps everything.
- **A restored till stays linked to your account:** restoring a backup keeps this till's own connection to your Universal Till account, even when the backup came from another till.
- **Clear message when the app cannot start:** if the desktop app fails to start, it now explains what to do and where its logs are, instead of showing a blank white window.

## Fixed

- **Phone menu:** the Lock button in the phone menu now lines up with the other entries.
