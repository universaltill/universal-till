## Improved

- **Safer connection to the main till:** The key an additional till uses to talk to the main till is no longer stored inside the till's backups, so a backup copy cannot be used to connect to your main till. The main till also changes this key for each additional till from time to time, so an old copy of it stops working.
- **Status stays visible on phone cards:** On a phone, an import row with a warning, or one that will be skipped, now shows its status right on the card. You no longer need to tap More to see what is wrong.

## Fixed

- **Sale screen on a phone held sideways:** The sale screen now shows the basket lines, the totals and the whole Pay button. On a wider phone the basket sits beside the items and Pay; on a small one, Pay stays pinned to the bottom while the basket scrolls.
- **Restoring the main till:** Restoring the main till from a backup no longer locks out additional tills that joined after that backup was made. They keep working without having to join again.
- **Restoring an additional till:** A restored additional till no longer carries the identity of the till it was copied from. It keeps selling, shows a "Join the main till again" notice, and once you join it again, its own entries made since the restore (such as no-sale drawer opens and cleared baskets) reach the main till under its own register instead of the original's.
- **Plugin download when the till is refused:** When the plugin store refuses the till's sign-in, the plugin page now shows the reason in your language and what to do, instead of a technical message in English.
