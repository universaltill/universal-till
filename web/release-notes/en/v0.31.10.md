## New

- **Remove a photo's background without any extra setup:** the item, category and variant photo pickers now offer **Remove background** on every till. It runs on the device itself and works offline, so you no longer need your own background-removal service; if you have one set up, the till still uses it. Nothing is processed until you tap it. On a small till it can take around 15 seconds.
- **AI assistants ask before they change anything:** an AI assistant plugin can only suggest a new item or a stock adjustment. The till shows you the change, and only applies it once you confirm. A cashier can't confirm it.

## Improved

- **You decide what an AI assistant may read:** an AI assistant plugin needs your permission for each kind of shop data it reads, and removing a permission takes effect straight away. New AI permissions are not switched on automatically when a plugin is installed or updated.
- **Plugin panels on the sell screen:** a plugin's button on the sell screen can now open the plugin's own panel next to the sale, without leaving it. A plugin can also show less to a cashier than to a manager.

## Fixed

- **Category names on phones:** on a phone, category tiles on the sell screen now sit two to a row, so a name like "Household" no longer breaks in the middle of the word.
- **Promotion percentages in your language's number format:** on a till set to a language that writes decimals with a comma (such as German or Turkish), a percent-off promotion now shows and pre-fills as 7,50 instead of 7.50.
