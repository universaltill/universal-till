## Improved

- **AI features come from the AI Assistant plugin:** identifying a product from a photo and **Ask your till** are now provided only by the AI Assistant plugin (version 2.0 or later), no longer also built into the till. If you use them, install or update that plugin. Background removal for item photos is unchanged.
- **Tills get an encrypted connection ready:** every till now also accepts encrypted connections on its shop-network port, using its own key that stays the same over time. This prepares additional tills to talk to the main till encrypted; nothing changes in how you work.

## Fixed

- **Takeaway rate overrides take a comma:** in a tax plugin's settings, the takeaway rate fields now accept `7,5` as well as `7.5`, work with the on-screen keyboard like the till's other percentage fields, and refuse entries that aren't a plain percentage.
- **A bad stored takeaway rate no longer blocks saving:** if a stored takeaway rate is out of range (0 % or less, or above 100 %), its field now shows empty with an "Invalid takeaway rate" note naming the stored value, and the rest of the settings page still saves. Before, it could stop the whole page from saving, or disappear on the next save without a word. A till using a comma for decimals now also shows saved rates with a comma.
- **Your own digits on the sale screen:** in Persian and Arabic, the card-fee hint, the pay-with-voucher button and split-payment messages now show amounts in the same digits as the rest of the basket.
- **User actions stay on screen on a 10-inch till:** on the Users page, a super admin's **Set PIN**, **Promote to super admin** and **Change role** controls no longer run off the edge of the list on narrower screens such as a 10-inch till; they wrap under their field instead.
- **Keyboard stays inside more dialogs:** in the dialogs for adding a table, editing an item, importing, tax codes, stock, the age check, shrinkage and the overflow list of categories, pressing Tab could move to buttons on the page behind the dialog. Tab now stays inside the dialog.
