## New

- **Full screen on Windows:** the Windows till now really goes full screen. In Fullscreen or Kiosk mode there is no title bar and the taskbar is hidden. Exit to OS window (with a manager PIN) or Alt+F4 always gets you back to the Windows desktop. A new installation opens full screen and starts when you sign in to Windows. An update keeps whatever you chose, and you can change both in Settings → Display.

## Improved

- **Plugins remove an erased customer too:** when you erase a customer for a privacy request, plugins that keep their own copy of that customer, such as loyalty or CRM connectors, are now told to delete it.
