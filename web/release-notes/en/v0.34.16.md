## Improved

- **Receive stock from my.:** the Adjust stock form in my. now lets you choose the stock location, whether it is a stock receipt or an adjustment, and an optional cost. The till records it as a normal stock movement.
- **Terms when you register:** registering a till with the cloud now asks you to tick that you accept the Universal Till cloud service terms, with a link to read them. Nothing is sent until you tick it.
