## New

- **Scan the pairing code:** When you add a till to your shop, you can now scan the QR code with the camera instead of typing the code.
- **Refund status in the Journal:** A refund now shows which sale it belongs to, and each sale shows whether it is Refunded, Partly refunded or has a refund pending.

## Improved

- **Backups:** When the offsite backup cannot connect, the till now says why the cloud refused it instead of a general error.
- **Reconnecting a till:** Reconnecting a till to your shop, or moving it to a new shop, is smoother and clearer about what will happen.
