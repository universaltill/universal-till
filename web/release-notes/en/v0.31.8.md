## Fixed

- **Identify by camera uses your own item photos:** when you point the camera at a product, the AI Assistant now compares it with the photos of items that actually have one, the ones confirmed at the till first and then the newest. Before, it only looked at the photos of the first items in alphabetical order, so in a larger catalogue an item further down the list, such as one named "Roasted almond…", could not be recognised from its own photo. Needs the AI Assistant plugin version 2.2 or later.
