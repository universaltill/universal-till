## Improved

- **Changes reach my. sooner:** after you edit an item, category or price on the till, the till now tells your my. account within seconds instead of waiting for the next check-in.
- **Receipts in the customer's language:** a receipt printed through the browser's print dialog now comes out in the language the customer picked.
- **Clearer pairing message:** when a till cannot pair with a shop because its cloud identity is fixed by its setup, it now says so in plain words.
- **Persian, Arabic and Turkish:** the category filter and the plan names are now shown correctly, and the manual covers the Android and Raspberry Pi first start.

## Fixed

- **Delete an item you never sold:** an item that was never sold and only has a removed-line record can now be deleted. Items with sales or real stock history are still kept, and the message now says why.
- **Import preview and import agree:** when the same item appears twice in one import file, the preview and the import now report the same problem.
