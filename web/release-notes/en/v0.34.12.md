## New

- **Stock levels in the online shop manager:** my.universaltill.com now has an **Inventory** page under **Manage shop**. It shows every stock-tracked item at the stock location you pick, and a manager can correct a quantity with a reason. Your main till applies the change.

## Improved

- **Gift voucher item in your language:** if your shop's language arrived after setup (for example, setup ran offline), the gift voucher item that setup created is now renamed into that language. An item you renamed yourself is left alone.
- **Plugin imports and exports in your language:** the till now tells import and export plugins which language you use, so plugins that support it reply in that language.
- **Lighter sales-report syncing:** the till no longer rebuilds the sales reports it sends to the cloud for past days that haven't changed.

## Fixed

- **Gift vouchers search:** searching for **MÜLLER** now finds *Müller*. Upper and lower case are ignored for accented letters too. The search and status also stay in the page address, so going Back from a voucher or reloading keeps them. On an empty list, the page no longer offers an export with nothing in it.
