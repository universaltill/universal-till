## Improved

- **Item icons on the basket:** an icon you set for an item on my.universaltill.com now also shows on its line in the basket, not only on the sale-screen tile and in item edit. An icon you apply from my.universaltill.com updates an item line that is already in an open basket.

## Fixed

- **Restoring a backup:** if the till stopped or lost power at the wrong moment while restoring a backup, it could start again with an empty database. The restore now finishes or is undone safely the next time the till starts, so your data is not lost.
