## Improved

- **Pairing a till with a shop:** The pairing code is easier to enter and a till on the same network is found more reliably, including the desktop app.
- **Problems in plain words:** When something goes wrong, the till now explains the problem and what to do next instead of showing a technical message.
- **Cloud sync status:** A small indicator shows whether this till is connected to your shop in the cloud.
- **Settings search:** Searching the settings now works from the first letter you type.
- **Plugins:** Installing, replacing and removing plugins is more reliable, and the App Store says clearly when a plugin cannot be installed.

## Fixed

- **Refunds:** A refund made without a return no longer leaves the sale in a wrong state.
- **Card payments:** A card payment that went through is now always recorded as a sale, also after the till is restarted mid-payment.
- **Repeated barcode scans:** Scanning the same barcode several times in a row is handled correctly.
- **Backups:** Backups no longer include secret keys.
- **Cash sales and receipts:** Signing and saving a cash sale is more robust, so a sale is not lost if something is interrupted.
