## Improved

- **Easier to tap:** text boxes and drop-down lists are now at least as tall as the buttons, so they are easier to hit with a finger on a touchscreen, including in kiosk mode.
- **Help matches the till:** the user manual now names Settings sections exactly as the till shows them in every language, such as "Data management".

## Fixed

- **Large text on a small window:** with a big display size on a narrow window, the sell screen no longer cuts off the scan buttons, the Pay row or the category "more" tab, and no longer scrolls sideways. The display size is now limited to what the window can hold.
- **Pay button:** Pay keeps its full label at a large display size, and the action buttons wrap onto a second row instead of shortening it.
- **Phone-sized lists:** on a narrow screen, a long column title now breaks only between words or at hyphens instead of in the middle of a word.
- **Data management wording:** messages that sent you to "Settings → Data" now name the section as it appears on screen, "Data management".
