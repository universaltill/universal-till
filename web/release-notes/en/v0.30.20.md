## New

- **Manage stock locations from the online shop manager:** you can now add, rename, retire and reactivate stock locations in the online shop manager (my.universaltill.com), and your main till applies the change. If the name you choose already belongs to a retired location, you are told which one and can reactivate it instead.

## Improved

- **Unpaid shops' tills keep their plan up to date without extra syncing:** a till that isn't on a paid plan now also checks in with the cloud when it is installed, after an update and the first time it starts each day. It doesn't keep retrying through the day, and otherwise it still doesn't sync.

## Fixed

- **Promotions fit a phone-sized screen:** on a phone-width till, editing a promotion no longer cuts controls off the edge of the screen — the fields wrap onto the next line and dates show the full year. The Kitchen stations page had the same problem and is fixed too.
- **The Subscription card shows the right date wording:** the Subscription card in Settings now says "Paid until" instead of "Renews", because the date shown is when the current paid period ends, whether or not the plan renews.
