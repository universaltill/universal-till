## Fixed

- **Revoked plugin permissions stay revoked:** updating a plugin from the marketplace, or reinstalling it, no longer switches back on a permission you had turned off for it.
- **VAT breakdown with a big sale discount:** when a sale discount was spread over several VAT rates and one rate had very little goods, that rate's share could exceed its goods and show a small negative amount on the receipt, invoice and Z report. A rate's share of the discount is now capped at its goods.
- **Recovery screen only on the till:** if the till can't start, its recovery screen (error details and the Retry button) now opens only on the till itself or on devices your installer has allowed, not on any device on the shop network.
- **Safe-mode link only where it works:** on an allowed second computer, the recovery screen no longer shows a safe-mode link that would refuse to open; it says safe mode can only be opened on the till itself.
- **No running-out warnings when you don't track stock:** with "Sell items without tracking stock" on, the till no longer predicts which items will run out — no running-out chip on Inventory or Reports, no order suggestions and no low-stock digest. Your stock quantities stay visible.
