## New

- **Phone sale screen:** on a phone the items now fill the screen as icons (the item's picture, or its colour with its initials). The menu is behind ☰ at the top, and a bar at the bottom shows Dine in / Takeaway and Pay with the total. Tap Pay to open the basket, change quantities and pay.
- **What's new:** Settings → About shows the installed version and what changed in it, and after an update managers see a one-time notice.
- **Settings as tiles:** Settings opens on large tiles (My shop, Selling, Payments, Receipts & printers, Staff & security and more), each with a short description.

## Improved

- **Permissions page:** actions are grouped (Sales, Catalog, Stock, Reports and so on), each with a description of what it unlocks.
- **Dialogs never block the till:** the status bar, Lock and exit stay reachable while a dialog is open.
- **Reorder list per location:** an item that has no stock at the chosen location but has stock elsewhere is listed as "Not stocked here".

## Fixed

- **iPhone and iPad app:** the version shows correctly instead of "dev".
- **Setting up a German shop:** the tax-rate plugin prompt shows even when the till is offline.
