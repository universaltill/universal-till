## New

- **New shops start with a gift voucher item:** finishing setup now adds a **Gift voucher** item, named in your shop's language (or "Gift voucher" if that language isn't installed yet), so you can sell vouchers straight away. It has no price, so the till asks for the value each time, and you can rename or hide it like any other item. Tills set up before this version keep their catalogue as it is.

## Improved

- **On-screen barcode for every voucher code:** the receipt on screen now shows a barcode for any gift voucher code, including pre-printed card numbers with lowercase letters or longer codes, not only for numbers the till creates.
- **More of the printed end-of-day report in your shop's language:** the totals, payment methods, tips and both VAT tables on the printed end-of-day report now follow your shop's language instead of always printing in English.

## Fixed

- **Your gift voucher item is kept while vouchers are open:** **Catalog cleanup**, and deleting an unused item in the online shop manager (my.universaltill.com), could remove the item you sell gift vouchers through, because selling a voucher doesn't count as selling the item. While your shop has any open gift voucher, that item is now kept; you can still switch it off.
- **Managers on a self-order till see everything they need:** when a manager signs in on a till in self-order mode, full-screen editing windows keep the status, **Lock** and exit buttons, and the "Updated to … — see what's new" notice shows once after an update. Customers' kiosk screens are unchanged.
- **Receipt details fit on a phone:** in the **Journal**, a receipt's **Refund**, **Print receipt** and **Journal** buttons now wrap below the title on a phone, and the receipt barcode shrinks to fit, so nothing runs off the screen.
- **Long problem messages fit on a phone:** in the **Back office**, a long line in **Recent problems** (such as a web address from a failed connection) now wraps instead of making the page wider than a phone screen.
- **Long barcodes fit the paper:** a printed barcode for a long code, such as a gift voucher card number on a receipt or a code on a label, could be wider than 80mm paper and come out missing or cut off. Barcodes are now printed more compactly so they fit; a code too long for any barcode is printed as text instead.
- **Additional tills reconnect to a restored main till:** when your main till is restored onto new hardware, your additional tills now recognise it and reconnect on their own, without pairing them again. A device that only pretends to be your main till is still refused, and the additional tills keep selling in the meantime.
