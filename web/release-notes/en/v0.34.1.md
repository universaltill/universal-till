## Improved

- **Journal shows gift vouchers:** a receipt in the journal now lists each gift voucher sold on it, with its number, holder and purpose (voided vouchers are marked), adds a vouchers-issued total, and names the voucher a payment used.
- **Import preview:** a gift voucher row is tagged Gift voucher in the import preview, before you import it.
- **Setup asks for the till's name:** the setup wizard no longer fills in "Till 1". Type a name for this till to continue.
- **Percentages in your language's format:** discount percentages and VAT rates on the sale screen, receipts, invoices and the tax report use your language's decimal separator (7,50% on a German till). The printed receipt's "Total incl. VAT" line is now translated.

## Fixed

- **Till screens open only on the till:** phones, tablets and PCs on the shop network now get an access-denied page instead of the till's screens. If you manage the till from a second device, ask your installer or support to allow it (setting UT_UI_ALLOW_CIDRS). Tills in the same shop still sync with each other, and customer guest pages keep working.
- **VAT breakdown off by one cent:** when a sale discount was spread over VAT rates and one rate had no goods left, the leftover cent could land on that rate, so the Z report and invoice showed it one cent off.
