## New

- **Messages from Universal Till:** A till can now show short messages from Universal Till: a bell with an inbox, and a pop-up for urgent ones. It is off by default, so nothing changes until it is switched on.
- **Backups are tidied up for you:** The till keeps the last 7 days of backups (you can choose 1 to 365 days) and removes older ones after each new backup. Backups you uploaded, the newest backup and the safety copy made before a restore are never removed. Each backup in the list also has a Delete button, with a confirmation, except the newest. Only managers and admins can delete.
- **Currency shown as a code:** A new Currency display setting lets a shop show amounts with the currency code (for example AED) or the symbol. Automatic prints the code for currencies written in Arabic script, such as AED, SAR, IQD, AFN and IRR. It applies on the screen, receipts, invoices and reports, and a receipt printer that cannot print the symbol uses the code. Only managers and admins can change it.
- **Next steps after setting up from your shop account:** After a till is set up from your shop's cloud account, a manager sees a card on the sale screen listing what to do next: staff PINs, card terminal and printer, and counting stock. Where the till holds the product list, the first step says that products were not copied. Close it when you are done.

## Improved

- **Safer connection to the main till:** The key an additional till uses to talk to the main till is no longer stored inside the till's backups, so a backup copy cannot be used to connect to your main till. The main till also changes this key for each additional till from time to time, so an old copy of it stops working.
- **Status stays visible on phone cards:** On a phone, an import row with a warning, or one that will be skipped, now shows its status right on the card. You no longer need to tap More to see what is wrong.
- **Pairing requests show up at once:** When another till asks to join, the request now appears on the main till's Tills page within a second or two, without waiting for the page to refresh.
- **Confirmation before replacing a till's cloud connection:** Pairing a till that is already connected to a shop now asks you to confirm first and names the shop, so a mistyped code can't cut the till off by accident. Sales, products and settings stay on the till either way.
- **Users and permissions from Settings:** Settings → Staff & security now links straight to Users (where PINs are set) and Permissions. Each link shows only to people who may open that page.
- **Photos in the self-order cart:** Each line in the self-order cart now shows the item's picture, the same one as on its button. If you change the picture while a customer is ordering, their cart shows the new one when it next updates.
- **Item photos on Android:** In the Android app, Take a Photo now opens the device's own camera app. The photo is resized for you and saved as soon as you accept it, or once you choose whether to remove its background.
- **Settings on an upright tablet:** Up to 900 pixels wide, Settings shows the list of sections first and then the section you pick at full width, as on a phone. On a tablet held upright the section no longer sits in a narrow panel.
- **Warnings are easier to read:** Warning text on its coloured background is now clearer in every theme, including the "not set" value on the Import page.
- **Better for screen readers and keyboards:** A wrong-PIN message and the error banners on the sign-in, setup and other pages are now announced by screen readers. The edit buttons in the promotions list have names, and on an item's Modifiers tab keyboard focus stays in place after you remove, attach or skip a group.
- **Promotions in one dialog:** Editing a promotion now opens one dialog, the same as for other lists. The table no longer scrolls sideways, so Status and the edit button are always on screen, even on a small kiosk display.
- **Setup pickers by keyboard:** On the setup page, the country and shop-type tiles can be chosen with the arrow keys, Home and End, and are announced properly by screen readers. The focus outline is also easier to see.
- **Easier to touch:** The close and lock buttons on record dialogs are now at least 44 pixels, and the border of the action column in lists no longer looks jagged on multi-line rows.
- **Your language installs itself:** A main till set to Arabic, Persian, Turkish or English now fetches its latest language update by itself when it is online. If it cannot, the language already on the till keeps working.

## Fixed

- **Sale screen on phones and short tablets:** The sale screen now shows the basket lines, the totals and the whole Pay button on a phone held sideways. On a wider phone the basket sits beside the items and Pay; on a small one, Pay stays pinned to the bottom while the basket scrolls. On short tablet windows the grand Total line is no longer cut off, and the buttons below the basket line up in tidy rows instead of one button wrapping alone.
- **Restoring the main till:** Restoring the main till from a backup no longer locks out additional tills that joined after that backup was made. They keep working without having to join again.
- **Restoring an additional till:** A restored additional till no longer carries the identity of the till it was copied from. It keeps selling, shows a "Join the main till again" notice, and once you join it again, its own entries made since the restore (such as no-sale drawer opens and cleared baskets) reach the main till under its own register instead of the original's.
- **Plugin download when the till is refused:** When the plugin store refuses the till, the plugin page now shows the reason in your language and what to do, instead of a technical message in English. This covers a till that was replaced by another till or moved to another shop, a shop with no owner yet, and a shop where plugin downloads are not available.
- **Confirm and PIN prompts:** Escape now cancels a confirmation or PIN prompt, focus returns to the button you pressed, and any message the prompt left behind is cleared. While a confirmation or PIN is being checked, Cancel and Escape wait, so the prompt can't be closed half-way or pop up again.
- **PIN prompt and the on-screen keyboard:** The keyboard button now appears on the manager-PIN prompt that some actions open. On a small touchscreen, Cancel and Confirm stay above the on-screen keyboard instead of hiding behind it.
- **Settings links on a phone:** Jumping straight to a section of Settings no longer hides its heading behind the top bar.
- **Quick Buttons:** When a category move is refused, the reason now shows on the row you moved instead of at the top of a list you may have scrolled past.
- **Lowering a quantity needs the manager PIN:** Pressing minus on a basket line, typing a smaller quantity or setting it to 0 now asks a cashier for a manager PIN, just like removing the line, and records the void. Raising a quantity, discounts and gift vouchers are not affected. An empty or invalid quantity is refused instead of silently removing the line.
- **When the till does not answer:** If the till does not answer within 30 seconds, a confirmation or PIN prompt or a button stops waiting and tells you the action may or may not have been done, so check before trying again. Long actions such as card payments, backups and uploads are never cut off.
- **Large sales queue on an additional till:** When the main till refuses a batch of sales as too big, the additional till now sends smaller batches until they fit, instead of retrying the same batch for ever. If a single sale is still refused, you see a warning and nothing is skipped.
- **Replacement main till receipts:** A main till that replaces a lost one no longer repeats the old till's receipt and invoice numbers. It uses its own prefix, which survives restoring a backup.
- **Reports:** On the Reports page, the Custom range option that covers the last day now reads Last 24 hours, so it is no longer confused with the business-day Today button.
- **Plugin menu:** One damaged plugin menu entry can no longer stop the till from starting.
