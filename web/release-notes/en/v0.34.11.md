## New

- **Add a language from Settings:** Settings → Language now also lists **More languages (download)** — languages this till doesn't have yet. Pick one and apply: it downloads in the background, the till keeps working, and it switches language once the download has arrived. The list appears only while the till is online.

## Improved

- **Printed Z-report in your language:** the remaining English sections of the printed end-of-day report — departments, tills, article groups, order types, vouchers, voids, cash count and the receipt range — now print in the shop's language.
- **Payment names in your language:** the payment methods in the Split payment tab, on the on-screen receipt, in the journal and in the Payments report now show their translated names (for example "Cash") instead of internal codes such as "cash" or "gift".

## Fixed

- **Line discounts larger than the line:** the basket now refuses a discount bigger than the line's total and says the most it can be, right under the item. Before, the sale failed at payment with a general error.
- **ID check sheet:** when the ID check opens before payment, pressing Enter no longer jumps to the help page.
- **Portrait and phone screens:** some dialogs, the payment panel and Settings no longer cut off buttons or scroll sideways on portrait tablets and phones.
