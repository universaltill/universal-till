## New

- **Gift vouchers page:** Menu → Gift vouchers lists every voucher sold, with its holder, value, balance and status, and shows each voucher's history with links to its receipts. Search by number or holder, filter by status, export to a spreadsheet (CSV), and import vouchers here. Managers and admins only.
- **Gift vouchers from your old till's backup:** when you import a backup (.bkp), gift voucher products arrive as Gift voucher items. Items you already imported can be switched with Catalog → Type.

## Improved

- **Registers follow your tills:** a register is now named after its till and is renamed with it. The Registers page lists active registers first and groups inactive ones below.

## Fixed

- **Order tracking QR:** removed from the kiosk and from receipts until order tracking moves to our cloud. The QR pointed at the till's own network address, which customers' phones can't open and shouldn't see. In the receipt designer the option is greyed out until then.
