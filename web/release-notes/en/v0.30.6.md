## Improved

- **Category pop-up on the sell screen:** it now opens full screen, so there is more room to find an item, and it closes by itself once the item is in the basket.
- **Item photos:** the camera preview for an item's picture is smaller and stays in view. When you take a photo it appears straight away and is saved at once — no second tap on "Upload Image".
- **Tidier storage:** once a day the till deletes files it no longer needs — old restore copies, unsent problem reports older than a week and leftover update downloads. Your sales, receipts and reports are never touched.

## Fixed

- **Report a problem:** cashiers no longer see a "View my reports" link that only managers can open.
