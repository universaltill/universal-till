## New

- **Remove background on a saved photo:** an item or category that already has a photo now has a "Remove background" button next to it, so you can clean up a photo you took earlier. "Use original photo" leaves the saved photo as it was.

## Fixed

- **Photo changed during a sale:** if you add, replace or remove an item's photo while that item is in the open sale, the basket now shows the new photo straight away instead of only on the next sale.
- **Held sales show the current photo:** a held sale brought back after an item's photo was changed or removed now shows the item's current photo, or none, instead of the old one or a broken image.
