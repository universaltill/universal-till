## New

- **Rename a till from the cloud:** renaming a till in the online shop manager (my.universaltill.com) now changes the name on that till too.

## Improved

- **Order boards and kitchen displays:** after a while without use, an order board or kitchen display keeps showing and updating orders, but anything else (Settings, refunds, the sale screen) asks for a PIN first. Kitchen screens no longer lock in the middle of service.
- **Joined tills:** while the main till can't be reached, Settings on a joined till shows the shop-wide settings read-only, with a note saying why. Settings that belong to that till alone stay editable.
- **Joined tills:** an open sale screen on a joined till no longer reloads its item grid when a sync only changes something unrelated, such as a setting or a PIN.
- **Stability:** if a background task such as a sync or a device scan hits an unexpected error, the till now records it in its log (with passwords and keys hidden) and carries on, instead of shutting down.

## Fixed

- Renaming a joined till in its own Settings no longer renames the main till.
- A joined till no longer says it isn't registered with the cloud when its main till is.
- The shop's default tax rate can now have decimals, for example 8.1 %.
- A till waiting for a quiet moment to restart after an update no longer warns that it needs a restart while a sale is still open.
