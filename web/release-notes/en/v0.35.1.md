## New

- **Support partner help:** Help has a new "Support partner" topic. It explains what a support partner is, where you connect or disconnect one on my.universaltill.com, and what the partner can and cannot see. On a till linked to your account it shows a QR code that opens that screen.

## Improved

- **Joining a shop with products:** a till that joins a shop with products now shows "Syncing your shop" while it loads the catalogue, then opens the sale screen. It no longer offers the empty-shop import welcome.
- **Catalogue items that arrive out of order:** an item that arrives before the category or other item it refers to is now applied once the rest has arrived, instead of being refused for good. This affected some barcodes.
- **Selling without internet:** on Wi-Fi with no internet, a sale no longer waits for an online fiscal signer that cannot answer. A signer that needs no network is still asked.
- **Low-storage warning on big disks:** the "Storage almost full" warning now starts at 5 GB free at most, instead of 10% of the disk. A large disk with plenty of space left no longer shows the warning.
- **Camera windows:** the barcode scanner and camera windows on the sale screen no longer cover the status bar.
- **Start-up problems on Windows:** if the desktop app cannot start and Windows has no WebView2, the explanation now opens in your browser, in your Windows language.
- **Local Plus plan:** the till now knows the Local Plus plan and which features it includes.

## Fixed

- **Sales sent to the main till:** a sale made in the same second as the end of a sending batch could be left out of the copy sent to the main till. Every sale is now sent.
- **"Attention needed" after pairing:** a failed attempt to pair a till no longer stays in the "Attention needed" list after a later pairing succeeds.
- **Order tracking help:** Help now says correctly that the customer tracking QR code is part of the Shop plan and above, and not of Local Plus.
