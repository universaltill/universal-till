## Fixed

- **First-run setup, Start selling:** The setup screen now says that the administrator PIN must be 4 to 8 digits, checks it before you move on, and tells you what to fix if something is wrong. Before, "Start selling" could do nothing when the PIN was not valid.
- **First-run setup, saving:** If setup cannot save your settings, the screen now shows a clear message and what to do, instead of a bare error page.
- **First-run setup, small screens:** The Back and Next buttons stay in view on laptops with a scaled display, and Enter now moves to the next step.
