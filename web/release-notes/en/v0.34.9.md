## New

- **Translate mode:** managers can see which text key a label on the till comes from, and fix it at the source. Turn it on under **Menu → Translations**. Hold **Alt** and click a text, press and hold it, or press **Alt+Enter**. A side panel shows the text's details, an **Open in Weblate** link with a QR code, and a box for changing the wording on this till only. It is never available on a self-order kiosk or in the demo.

## Improved

- **Settings → Tills shows each till's role:** the card now starts with the till's role: main till, additional till or satellite. An additional till or satellite also shows the address of its main till. The register picker is now called **Cash register for payouts** and, if you can manage registers, links to the Registers page. Throughout the till and the manual, "primary till" now reads **main till**.

## Fixed

- **No blank strip under order cards on a phone:** on a phone-sized screen, order cards and other lists no longer show an empty strip at the bottom of each card.
