**Branch:** `fix/5099-disk-floor-cap` · **Author:** Opus (routine `:41`) ·
**Reviewer:** Sonnet subagent, fresh context, isolated worktree

## What shipped

- `diskspace.Floor` is now `max(500 MiB, min(10% of the disk, 5 GiB))`
  (new `MaxPercentFloorBytes`). An owner's 926 GB disk with 58 GB free (6%)
  had a 92 GB floor, so it showed **Storage almost full** and raised a
  `disk.low` Problem that my. listed under "Attention needed".
- Every consumer reads the same function: the status-bar chip, the
  low-disk housekeeping sweep, the `disk.low` Problem, and
  `backupUploadCap`. They stay consistent, and the change needs no
  cloud-side work.
- Small till disks are unchanged: up to 50 GiB the 10% part still decides.
  A 32 GiB SD card keeps a 3.2 GiB floor.
- Help topic `backups` (en/de/tr/ar/fa) now says "(at most 5 GB)". The
  chip and status-bar template comments are updated too.
