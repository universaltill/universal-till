# Review: shell Back — history first, declared parent as fallback (ut-docs#3352, ADR-0137)

- **Branch**: `feat/3352-shell-back`
- **Author**: Opus 5.5 (pipeline lane `lane:cloud-24`)
- **Reviewer**: Fable, as an independent subagent with a fresh context. The card is `complexity:hard`, and the reviewer was a different model from the author.
- **Date**: 2026-10-02

## What shipped

- `internal/uislot/parent.go` `ParentOf(path)` works out where Back goes when there is no earlier page in the app to return to. It reads the slot tables:
  - `/menu` → `/`
  - an `CoreItems` section → `/items`
  - an Administration-group entry → `/admin`
  - any other Menu or rail entry → `/menu`
  - a sub-page → the nearest declared page above it
  - anything else → `/menu`
- `internal/httpx`: a per-request `backHref` template func, bound in `withHelpHref` next to `helpHref`.
- `web/ui/partials/nav.html`: one `a.nav-back` on every nav page except Sell. On the rail it sits under the logo. On a phone it comes first in the top bar, before ☰.
  - `app.css`: phone placement, a mirrored arrow in RTL, and no tile background, because Back is an action, not a destination.
- `web/public/app.js` `utNavBack`:
  - Every history entry gets an index in `history.state.utNav`. It is set through wrapped `pushState`/`replaceState` and on `hashchange`.
  - A full load continues the count only when its referrer is on the same origin.
  - A tap is `history.back()` when the index is above 0. Otherwise the link's `href` (the parent) is followed.
  - If no `popstate`, `beforeunload` or `pagehide` arrives within 1.5 s, the parent is used.
- Manual: a "Going back" section in `web/help/{en,de,ar,fa,tr}/menu.md`, and screenshots regenerated with `make docs-shots`. The README gains a Navigation section.
- Tests:
  - `uislot.TestParentOf` and `TestParentOf_EveryDeclaredEntry`.
  - `httpx.TestNav_BackLinkHrefIsTheDeclaredParent` and `TestNav_NoBackLinkOnSell`.
  - `e2e/tests/nav-back-3352.spec.ts`, 12 tests.
  - `nav-rail-svg-icons-lock-1423.spec.ts` updated for the twelfth rail icon and Back's deliberately transparent background.

No new locale keys: Back reuses `common.back`.

## Review findings

| # | Severity | Finding | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | blocker | `nav-rail-svg-icons-lock-1423` (auth project) still expected 11 icons and the same background on every rail item. | **Fixed.** The spec now expects 12 icons. Back's width is checked, and Back is left out of the background-parity check. |
| 2 | major | Back could leave the app. `ut.navIdx` survived a visit to another site in the same tab, so a typed URL or bookmark continued the count, and Back went to the other site (reproduced). | **Fixed.** An unstamped full load continues the count only from a same-origin referrer, capped by `history.length`. New e2e test: "a page arrived at from another site …". It fails on the pre-fix script. |
| 3 | major | The 700 ms fallback could cut short a slow cross-document traverse on WebKit (Pi, iOS). `popstate`/`pagehide` arrive too late for that. | **Fixed.** `beforeunload` now counts as "left" because it fires before the fetch, and the timer is 1.5 s. Not verifiable on WebKit here (no WebKit binary). |
| 4 | major | Entries made outside htmx had no index: Settings `#cat-…` links and its own `pushState`. Back from a category pushed `/menu`, a new entry, instead of returning to the grid. | **Fixed.** `pushState` is wrapped and `hashchange` stamps the entry. The `htmx:pushedIntoHistory` listener was removed because the wrapper covers it. New e2e test for Settings categories. It fails on the pre-fix script. |
| 5 | minor | `location.replace` counted one entry too many. | **Fixed** by the `history.length` cap and the referrer rule. |
| 6 | minor | The ADR's claim about motion was wrong: a traverse is htmx's reload, which runs no transition. | **ADR text corrected.** Behaviour is unchanged and matches browser Back today. |
| 7 | minor | `ParentOf` ignores a plugin regrouping a tile into Administration. | **Accepted.** Recorded as a known limit in ADR-0137. |
| 8 | minor | The fallback from Inventory goes to Items, which needs `catalog_management`; a custom role with stock access only would see a 403. | **Accepted.** Recorded in ADR-0137. No built-in role splits these two permissions. |
| 9 | minor | At 1024×600 the rail grows by about 53px, so a manager's Lock sits further below the fold. That rail already scrolls today. | **Accepted.** Recorded in ADR-0137. |
| 10–11 | nit | The spec was missing two cases, and the map's `/settings` row was wrong. | **Fixed.** |

The reviewer also checked these and found them fine:
- Login, setup, self-order and order-tracking pages have no Back, because they have no nav.
- The `replaceState` wrapper works with htmx and with `settings.html`.
- No `.ut-nav-pending` marker is left behind after a Back.
- The phone bar fits at 320/360px with alert chips showing.
