# Review — plugin jobs, poll component, job_progress (ut-docs#3908)

**Change:** ADR-0121 build 9b (§3 `job_progress`, §7 `poll`, §8 long calls).
Branch `feat/3908-plugin-jobs`. Built by Opus 5.5 (dev subagent). Reviewed
independently by Fable.

## What shipped

- **Answer shape.** A `ui.action.ask` answer may now be
  `{"job":{"event":"<plugin-id>.<name>"}}`.
  - The event must be in the plugin's own namespace (#3329) and at most 256 bytes.
  - A job's own answer may be a document or a redirect. It may not be another job.
  - Decoders: `pluginview.ActionAnswer` and `DecodeJobResult`.
- **Job registry.** Core answers at once with its own `poll` component and
  runs the event off the request path (`internal/pages/plugin_job.go`).
  - The poll is `GET <route>?_job=<id>`, about once a second. It shows a progress bar, the plugin's message and a no-JS "Check progress" link.
  - The id is 128 random bits and is bound to the plugin and its route.
- **Runtime (`plugins.WithJob`).**
  - The deadline is `limits.long_call_s` (at most 300 s).
  - `salePath=false` whatever the event name, so a job never takes the reserved slot.
  - A job answer is capped at the `ui.*` answer cap (1 MiB).
  - A progress sink feeds the new ABI-3 host function `job_progress(pct, key)`. It returns -1 outside a job, and -4 for a key over 256 bytes, unreadable, not UTF-8, or not in the plugin's own bundle.
- **Caps (`plugins.JobCaps`).**
  - Per plugin: `min(2, perPlugin-1)`, which is 2 on desktop and 1 on Android/iOS.
  - Across plugins: `(global-1)/2`, which is 7 on desktop and 3 on mobile.
  - A job over a cap gets the translated `plugin.job.busy` notice and HTTP 429.
- **Lifetime.**
  - A job nobody polls for 15 s is cancelled, which frees its slot.
  - A result nobody fetches is dropped after 30 s, and at most 4 unfetched results are kept per plugin.
  - A result is handed out once; after that, and for an unknown job, `plugin.job.gone` is shown.
  - A failed or timed-out job shows `plugin.view.unavailable`.
- **Poll responses.** An htmx poll with no change gets `204`, so the live region isn't re-announced. A no-JS poll always gets the full page.
- **`_job` is reserved.** It is never forwarded to the plugin as a param.
- **i18n and docs.**
  - `plugin.job.{running,busy,gone,refresh}` in en/ar/fa/tr.
  - The help topic `plugins.md` in all 5 languages.
  - `docs/plugin_guidelines.md`.
  - ut-docs `reference/plugin-views.md` and `reference/plugin-host-functions.md`.

## Findings (Fable)

| # | Severity | Finding | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | major | A cap of 2 jobs per plugin was more than the call gate allows on mobile (1 ordinary slot). Job 2 would wait for the slot with its deadline running and block the plugin's own page asks. There was also no cap across plugins, so 4 plugins × 2 jobs used up the ordinary global slots on mobile. | **Fixed.** The caps now come from the gate (`JobCaps`): 1 per plugin on mobile and 2 on desktop, plus a cap across plugins. The busy wording no longer names a count. Tests: `TestJobCaps_FollowCallGate_3908`, `TestPluginJob_MobileCapIsOne_3908`, `TestPluginJob_GlobalCap_3908`. |
| 2 | major (a11y) | The poll's `role=status` live region was replaced every second, so a screen reader re-announced it at 1 Hz. | **Fixed.** An unchanged progress now gets 204, so htmx swaps nothing. Test: `TestPluginJob_UnchangedPollIs204_3908`. |
| 3 | minor | `_job` was silently taken over on plugin-page GETs. | **Fixed.** It is stripped from params and documented. Test: `TestPluginView_JobParamReserved_3908`. |
| 4 | minor | There was no limit on how many unfetched results were kept. | **Fixed.** At most 4 per plugin; the oldest is dropped. Test: `TestPluginJob_UnfetchedResultsCapped_3908`. |
| 5 | minor | `job_progress` has no rate limit and takes the registry mutex. | **Accepted.** The cost is bounded: one short critical section per call, on the plugin's own job, and Go's starvation mode keeps the mutex fair. A per-plugin token bucket is not worth its code until a real plugin spins on it. |
| 6 | nit | The plugin check in `poll()` duplicates the route check, because routes are unique across plugins. | **Accepted** as defence in depth. |
| 7 | nit | A poll GET consumes the result. | **Accepted.** It sets `no-store`, and a HEAD poll does not consume. |
| 8 | nit | `ui:page` is checked only when the action starts, so a permission revoked mid-job still lets the result through. | **Accepted.** The window is at most `long_call_s`, and the result can only be that plugin's own validated document. |
| 9 | nit | The new `en.json` keys need follow-up PRs in the language packs. | **Done in this cycle** (de/es/pt pack PRs). |

## Found by CI after the review

- `internal/auth` `TestEveryPollerIsClassified` failed: the poll's templated `hx-get` was not classified as a background poll. Left like that, the 1 s job poll would have kept an untouched till signed in for up to 5 minutes and defeated the idle auto-lock (ut-docs#2901).
- **Fixed:** `auth.pluginJobPoll(r)` treats a GET/HEAD of a `/plugin/…` route that carries `_job` as no-touch. It is matched per request, because the path is the plugin's own route.
- **Tests:**
  - `TestPluginJobPollNeverExtendsTheSession`: the poll doesn't touch the session, but the plugin page, a plain query and a POST action do.
  - The poller guard checks the template through `requestPollers`.
- **Red first:** with a stub returning false, both tests fail.

## Verified beyond the automated tests

- **Mutation checks by the reviewer:**
  - Removing `salePath=false` makes `TestWasmJob_NeverSalePath_3908` fail.
  - Removing the per-plugin cap makes `TestPluginJob_ThirdJobBusy_3908` fail.
  - Removing the route binding makes `TestPluginJob_OnlyOwnRoute_3908` fail.
- **Mutation checks by the orchestrator:**
  - Changing the 204 to 200 makes `TestPluginJob_UnchangedPollIs204_3908` fail ("unchanged first poll = 200, want 204"). Restored, it passes.
- **Mutation checks by the dev:** the global-cap check and the unfetched cap were each removed, and their tests failed.
- **A real wasm guest:** `TestWasmJob_UsesJobDeadline_3908`. A guest sleeping 600 ms fails at the 200 ms event deadline and completes under a job.
- **Visual check:** the poll (40 %, message), busy and gone states were rendered through the real handler and templates and screenshotted at 1024×600 and 360 px, and the screenshots were looked at.
  - The poll sits in the page body; the nav rail, status bar and lock stay reachable, and there is no modal.
  - The bar is full width and the refresh link is at the 46 px touch floor.
  - The layout uses logical CSS.
  - Not checked on a physical tablet or with a real screen reader. The 204 fix is reasoned from htmx 1.9.12's swap rule and covered by a unit test.
