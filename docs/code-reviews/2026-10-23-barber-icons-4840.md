## Tester / UX pass
- Four own glyphs redrawn in the 24-unit, 2px, round-cap style: hairbrush
  (flat paddle head with three bristle bars), shaving-brush (lathered dome,
  band, tapered handle), cape (collar plus gown), trimmer (T-head, guard
  teeth, taper). Tiles regenerated (`TestCategoryIconTiles_InSync`).
- Checked by eye at 128/64/28/20 px (rsvg contact sheet). hairbrush and
  shaving-brush now read clearly down to 28px and no longer look like the
  mirror or a bulb.
- UX1: `.builtin-icon-tile` gets `overflow-wrap: anywhere; min-inline-size: 0`
  so "Haarschneidemaschine" wraps inside the tile (RTL-safe, no physical
  properties). Proposal for the de pack (S3): "Haarschneider" for clippers and
  "Ersatzteile & Service" for spare-parts (recorded in labels4840.py).
- Verdict: PASS for S1 with the weak spots below; docs-shots on Linux still due.

## Known weak art
- `cape`: reads as a gown or bib, acceptable with its label, not obviously a barber cape.
- `trimmer`: at 20px resembles a razor or flag; clippers beside it disambiguates.
- `hairbrush`: paddle head is better than before but still brush-generic.
- `tea tree` to shampoo is a judgement call (tea tree oil is also sold as a drink in cafes; barber context assumed).
