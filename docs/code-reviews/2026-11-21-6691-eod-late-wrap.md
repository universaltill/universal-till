# Z late note: paper wraps, screen table scrolls in its box, blank Sales (ut-docs#6691)

Branch `fix/6691-eod-late-wrap`. Covers the two #5776 follow-ups on ut-docs#6691
(findings 7 and 8 of `2026-11-19-eod-late-note-5776.md`); the three #5852 stock-take
items stay open on the card. Built by Sonnet, tested and reviewed independently by Opus.

## What changed
- Paper (`internal/pages/eod_late_note.go`): the three explanation lines, each till label
  and the count-only cleared label word-wrap at `print.Width` (42) instead of being clipped
  (`print.WrapWords`, now exported from `internal/print/width.go`). Wrapped cleared rows
  keep their 2-space indent. Amount rows (`footerRow`) are unchanged, so an amount is never
  cut; English output is byte-identical.
- Screen (`web/ui/partials/reports_tab_eod.html`, `web/public/app.css`): the late table sits
  in `.eod-late-scroll` (logical CSS; `inline-size:0; min-inline-size:100%` so the outer
  cell cannot grow), and at <=480px a reset scoped to `.eod-late-scroll` keeps it a normal
  table instead of the phone-card layout. Sales is blank for a till with no late sales
  (`SalesCount`), shows 0.00 for a real zero-total sale, and the column is hidden when no
  till had late sales (`HasSales`).
- `web/i18n-meta/en.meta.json`: `reports.zpaper.late_title|late_made_before|late_included`
  `max_length` 42 -> 84, descriptions say they wrap. No `en.json` text change, no new keys,
  no translations.
- Help `web/help/en/reports.md`: one sentence (wrap, blank cell, hidden column).
  `web/help/img/manifest.json`: surface hash via `update-docs-shots-surface-hash.sh` and the
  en/reports topic hash re-pinned; no screenshotted page shows late entries, no pixel change.
