# Review: low-disk floor — prune early, cap backups, status-bar chip (ut-docs#3121)

**Author:** Opus 5.5 (lane:cloud-24) · **Reviewer:** Fable, independent subagent in a separate worktree

## What shipped

- `internal/diskspace` (new): `Probe(dir)` — statfs on Linux/Android
  (`f_frsize` units, falls back to `f_bsize`), statfs on macOS/iOS/FreeBSD,
  `GetDiskFreeSpaceEx` on Windows (quota-aware free bytes); other platforms
  return `ErrUnsupported`, treated as "unknown" (never prune or warn on a
  guess). `Floor(total) = max(500 MB, total/10)`; `Low(u)` is free < floor.
- `internal/housekeeping`: `Limits` with `Normal` (the existing retention
  table) and `LowDisk` (newest 3 backups; newest 1 restore copy, none older
  than 1 day — the statutory floor still binds; unsent bug reports and
  update downloads older than 1 day; staged uploads unchanged because a
  younger one may be in use). `Run` became `RunWith(…, Limits)`.
  `CapBackups(dbPath, free)` keeps the snapshots within 25% of the space
  they could release (free + their own size), oldest first, never fewer
  than 3 (AC 4, X = 25%). Retention table rows updated.
- `internal/server.runHousekeeping` (hourly): probes the data directory's
  disk; below the floor it runs the `LowDisk` sweep at once, whatever the
  daily schedule; every hour it applies `CapBackups`; a disk still low after
  the clean-up raises the keyed Problem `disk.low` (Problems panel + cloud
  heartbeat), one per episode, resolved once the disk is above the floor.
- `GET /ui/disk-space-chip` + `disk_space_chip.html`: "Storage almost full"
  amber chip in the status bar for viewers who may open Settings
  (admin/manager), linking to Settings → Backups; empty for cashiers and
  when free space is unknown. Never a modal; selling is untouched. Shown on
  the phone sale screen too (added to the attention-chip `:has()` list).
  Registered in shell/background-poll paths and the demo allow-list; new
  `hard-drive` icon (mirrored in `internal/uislot`).
- i18n: `status.disk_low.label` / `.hint` in en/ar/fa/tr; de/es/pt pack PRs
  follow core (new key → core merges first, ut-docs CLAUDE.md).
- Manual: `web/help/{en,de,tr,fa,ar}/backups.md` "Automatic clean-up"
  describes the backup cap, the low-disk floor and the chip.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | major | A process-global "already reported" bool went stale once 50 later warnings evicted the `disk.low` entry from the Problems ring, so a long low-disk episode vanished from the panel/heartbeat. | **Fixed:** `reportDiskLow` checks the ring (`logging.OpenProblems`) instead; test logs 60 filler warnings and asserts the Problem is raised again. |
| 2 | minor | FreeBSD `f_bavail` is signed; a negative value wrapped to a huge free figure. | **Fixed:** clamped to 0. |
| 3 | minor | A persistent probe error left an open `disk.low` Problem open forever. | **Fixed:** an unmeasurable disk resolves it. |
| 4 | nit | "low disk" INFO line every hour during an episode. | **Fixed:** logged only on the first low sweep of an episode. |
| 5 | nit | Help says "a quarter of the free disk space" (code: free + backups) and "the newest copy set aside by a restore" (code: newest 1, ≤1 day, outside the statutory floor). | Accepted: accurate in practice (statutory floor ≥ 10 years globally); plain-language simplification. |
| 6 | nit | Low-disk test doesn't seed update downloads. | Accepted: limit plumbing is shared; Normal-limit test covers the kind. |
| 7 | nit | Budget truncates before multiplying (≤ 99 bytes). | Accepted. |
| 8 | nit | Relative `UT_DB_PATH` makes the probe cwd-relative. | Accepted: pre-existing for every DBPath consumer; chip and housekeeping agree. |
