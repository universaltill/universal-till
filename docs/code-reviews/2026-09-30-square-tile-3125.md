## What shipped

- `internal/imaging/tile.go`: `TileSize = 512`, `ErrNoSubject`,
  `ErrBadTileSize` and `SquareTile(src, bg, size)`:
  - `size` outside `1..MaxThumbEdge` → `ErrBadTileSize` (so the output never
    exceeds `MaxThumbEdge` and passes through the thumbnail pipeline unchanged);
  - bounding box of pixels with 8-bit alpha ≥ 16 over `src.Bounds()` (any
    origin); none → `ErrNoSubject`;
  - crop, scale the longer edge to `round(size × 0.84)` and the shorter one
    rounded with a floor of 1 (same rounding rule as `DownscaleMaxEdge`);
  - centre on a `size × size` RGBA canvas filled with `bg` (`nil` =
    transparent), subject drawn with `CatmullRom` + `draw.Over`.
- No caller yet: #3127 (cutout endpoint) wires it. Until then the
  whole-program deadcode guard sees `SquareTile`/`subjectBounds` as
  unreachable, so both are added to `scripts/ci/deadcode-baseline.txt`;
  #3127 must remove those two lines (the guard does not fail when an entry
  disappears).

## Tests

14 unit tests on synthetic images (`tile_test.go`): off-centre subject
centred; tall/wide/square/upscale keep aspect with the exact target edge;
fully transparent and all-alpha<16 → `ErrNoSubject`; alpha 16 counts, 15
doesn't; opaque (and non-square) input squared whole; size bounds; bg fills
the margin (white / palette / transparent); positive and negative origins;
soft-edge blending over bg; exact rounding at size 128 (108×72); a 1-px-wide
subject keeps one column; PNG round-trip.

**TDD:** author saw the fail-first compile error (`undefined: SquareTile`);
reviewer re-verified in a worktree — with the body replaced by
`return nil, nil` every test fails alone; restored, all pass.

Each fix was re-verified by re-applying its mutation: all four now fail
their new test. Reviewer also confirmed: alpha threshold is exact (alpha is
never premultiplied), bbox half-open and correct at negative origins,
CatmullRom does not bleed pixels from outside the crop (clamped to `sr`),
Gray/YCbCr (JPEG) inputs square whole, paletted transparency crops, 1×1 and
`size` 1 / `MaxThumbEdge` work.
