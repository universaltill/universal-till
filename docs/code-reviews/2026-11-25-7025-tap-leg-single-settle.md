## What shipped
- `internal/pages/tap_attention.go`: the broker-first settle of `POST /api/pay/attempts/attention/resolve` moved unchanged into `settleAttentionAttempt` (ADR-0198 §6.8: broker asked first, "not charged" refused while the broker says paid, reason required on override, audit, native release). A successful settle now also appends journal `resolved` and answers `leg_resolved`; a journal failure is logged and the settle stands. The JSON list shows a tap payment once, under `attempts[].leg_state`; its leg leaves `legs` (additive otherwise).
- `internal/pages/leg_attention.go`: `POST /api/pay/legs/resolve` re-reads both records under the shared `tapLegInFlight` lock. A leg with an open attempt (a "tap row") needs a `decision` (409 `choose_outcome` without one) and settles through the same function, then appends `resolved`; a decision on a leg with no open attempt gets 409 `attempt_settled`. Other legs keep today's Mark as handled. Refusals map to card-specific keys.
- `web/ui/partials/payment_attention.html`, `web/public/app.css`: tap row with two equal secondary outcome buttons (Not charged / Charged — refund at the provider), optional reason, note; decision and reason ride through the existing non-modal PIN dialog. Enter in the note submits nothing (disabled hidden default button).
- `web/public/app.js` + `unbounded_routes_test.go`: `/api/pay/legs/resolve` joins the unbounded client paths (a slow broker can take ~45 s).
