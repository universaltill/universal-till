Tester: independent agent.

## What shipped

- `internal/catimport/placeholder_norm.go`: `normalizeWord` (and with it
  `FoldForSearch`) drops U+200C ZWNJ and U+200D ZWJ. They join or shape parts
  of one word and never mark a word boundary, so "هات‌داگ" and "هاتداگ" fold
  equal. `normalizedWords` stays the **split** form: it turns ZWNJ and ZWJ into
  spaces before folding, so `کیک‌هایی` still stems to cake. The new
  `joinedWords` gives the **joined** form, and `hasZWNJ` reports either joiner.
- `internal/catimport/placeholder_lang.go`: the auto-suggest matcher adds a
  joined row for every label with a joiner. When the item name has one, the
  matcher also tries the joined view of the name. Row order and ranking are
  unchanged; the extra rows only repeat the same icon.
- `web/public/icon-picker-filter.js` `fold`: drops U+200C/U+200D, the same as Go.
- Tests:
  - Go fold, `joinedWords`, ZWJ split, the fa matcher table (hot dog,
    breakfast, cake, bare `ها` stays generic) and the picker keywords.
  - Six new parity rows, plus node `wordMatches` cases.
  - A Persian case in `e2e/tests/icon-search-shop-languages-6042.spec.ts`
    (typed with ZWNJ, without it, and with a space).
- **Changed fixture row:** the existing `پنیر‌خامه` row in
  `testdata/search_fold_parity.json` now expects `پنيرخامه` (ZWNJ dropped).
  That is the intended new fold output.

The reviewer found no false matches or ranking regressions in the auto-suggest.
A differential fuzz of 20,000 strings found no Go/JS difference. The inputs had
joiners at the start, at the end, repeated and next to spaces, plus Hangul jamo,
Arabic letter variants and combining marks. Both folds are idempotent, and the
extra rows add negligible time to building the label table.
